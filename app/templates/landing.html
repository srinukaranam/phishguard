Core Capabilities

Built for SOC Analysts & Threat Hunters

Deep Email Parsing

Safely parses complex MIME structures, decodes RFC 2047 headers, and extracts text/HTML bodies without executing malicious payloads.

Explainable Detection

Modular rules evaluate Header spoofing, URL obfuscation, and dangerous attachments. Every finding is backed by raw evidence and a rule ID.

Threat Intel Enrichment

Asynchronously queries VirusTotal v3 for extracted IOCs, caching results in PostgreSQL to respect API rate limits and ensure fast investigations.

Transparent Risk Scoring

Findings are aggregated into a 0–100 score with correlated-indicator deduplication. Severity bands map directly to recommended SOC actions.

Case Management

Track investigations with status workflows, append analyst notes, and maintain a full audit trail of all actions taken during triage.

PDF Investigation Reports

Generate print-ready HTML reports containing findings, IOCs, intelligence attribution, and analyst conclusions for stakeholder review.
